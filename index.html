How we build

Every product here is built the same way: Claude Code designs, writes the core, reviews the work against primary sources, and keeps the records. The founder decides; the models do the engineering. Other models take mechanical tasks.

Products

1. Bilingual Manga Reader — next flagship, in development

A mobile app for language learners. The reader takes two language editions of the same manga that the user already reads, aligns the pages on the device, and lets the learner look at the other language through a movable lens over the page. No images are stored on our servers; everything stays on the user's device.

Done so far: the page-alignment core (Python, written by Claude) matches a real volume page by page with zero mismatches; Android app shell of 20,000+ lines; PC prototype running.

2. Impact-Structure Screening — research tooling, in development

Tools that apply one consistent processing to public terrain, gravity and magnetic surveys, calibrate it on known impact structures around the world, and screen all of Japan for candidates.

Done so far: processing reproduced on 28 known structures; the Japanese reference site (Oikeyama) matched tile for tile; 260+ commits.

3. Automated Sports Video Line — live, monetized

Our first product to reach revenue. The line turns overseas fan reactions to baseball and soccer games into narrated Japanese-language videos, with a human review step before anything is published.

Done so far: baseball channel monetized; 119 videos and 600,000+ views as of August 2026; soccer channel launched October 2026.

製品

1. バイリンガル漫画リーダー(開発中・次の主力): 読者が自分で開いた同じ漫画の 2 言語版を端末の中で頁ごとに重ね、動かせるレンズでもう一方の言語を見る語学学習アプリ。画像はサーバに置かず、端末の中だけで扱います。

これまで: 頁合わせの核(Python・Claude 作)が実物の 1 巻を誤対応 0 で合わせた。Android 版の器 2 万行超。PC 版の試作が動作。

2. 衝突痕の選別ツール(開発中・研究用): 公開されている地形・重力・磁気の観測に同じ処理を施し、世界の既知の衝突痕で確かめ、日本全土から候補を選別します。

これまで: 既知 28 構造で処理を再現。日本の基準地(御池山)がタイル単位で一致。260 コミット超。
