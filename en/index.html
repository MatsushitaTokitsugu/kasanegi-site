About Kasanegi

Kasanegi is a research and development studio based in Osaka, Japan. We work on three things: research to find undiscovered meteorite craters in Japan's terrain, a language-learning app that uses manga, and a content production system that earns revenue fully automatically.

Most of our research and development is done with Claude Code.

The name comes from kasanegi (重ね着), wearing kimono in layers. We layer software, data and AI onto geological knowledge, to carry out research and development. In the four months since we started, our three lines of work have passed 1,700 commits.

01 · Research

Searching for undiscovered meteorite craters

In Japan, Oikeyama in Nagano is known as a meteorite crater. Japan's steep, forested land is likely to hold craters that have not yet been found. We read the latest Japan-wide red relief map with AI instead of the human eye, to search the whole country for undiscovered craters.

For AI to recognise craters, we keep an inventory of 354 impact structures worldwide, including 226 confirmed sites, and have brought the terrain of 48 of them, from public 1 m data, into red relief maps on the same basis as Japan's. At Oikeyama we reproduce the Japan-wide red relief map to within one colour level, so terrain from around the world and from Japan is compared on one scale.

Finding crater candidates in an enormous number of terrain images is work that requires advanced AI. Our founder, a certified mineral appraiser, checks the candidates with a geologist's eye.

02 · App

kasanegi, a language app that uses manga

kasanegi layers the Japanese and English editions of the same manga page by page on your phone. Enter two URLs, and a whole episode is layered page by page. Move the round lens, and that part switches to the other language. Any manga you love becomes study material.

The two editions are matched page by page by their artwork and aligned. On real manga, pages are matched without error and layered as they are. Images never leave the device.

An Android app, a PC version with a round lens, and a demo you can try in your browser are all running.

03 · System

A fully automated content system that earns revenue

Our research and development cost more than $400 a month in AI subscriptions. As part of that work, we built a content production system with Claude Code in which AI handles everything from writing the script to editing and uploading, fully automatically.

Both YouTube channels run by the system are monetized, and the revenue exceeds what we pay for Claude. While much AI-generated video struggles with quality and accuracy, our videos, built from real events and real people's voices, have passed 1.9 million total views.

The two channels are one use of the system, and channels in other languages and genres run on the same system.
