About Kasanegi

Kasanegi is a research and development studio based in Osaka, Japan. We work on three things: research on meteorite impact structures using Japan-wide terrain data, a language-learning app that uses manga, and a system that produces content fully automatically.

Most of our research and development is done with Claude Code.

The name comes from kasanegi (重ね着), wearing kimono in layers. We layer software, data and AI onto geological knowledge, so that a small team can take on large research and development work.

PROJECTS

What we work on

01 · Research

Searching for Japan's first meteorite crater

No meteorite impact crater has been confirmed in Japan yet. We combine the Japan-wide red relief map released in 2026 with AI image analysis to search the whole country for candidates. We validate the method on impact craters confirmed around the world.

We think the same method can also be applied to terrain data abroad, to craters on the Moon, and to archaeological sites such as kofun burial mounds.

02 · App

kasanegi, a language app that uses manga

kasanegi shows the Japanese and English editions of the same manga layered page by page on your device. Move the round lens, and that part switches to the other language. Images never leave the device.

A demo you can try in your browser is available.

03 · System

A fully automated content system that earns revenue

Our research and development cost more than $400 a month in AI subscriptions. As part of that work, we built a production system with Claude Code in which AI writes the script, edits and uploads on its own. Both YouTube channels run by the system are monetized, and the revenue exceeds what we pay for Claude.

Much AI-generated video struggles with low quality and inaccurate content. Our system builds each video from match records and real fans' comments. What we learn here about getting AI to do reliable, high-quality work feeds back into our research and the language app.
