LANGUAGE APP · KASANEGI

Read the same manga in two languages, layered

kasanegi is an app for people learning a language. It shows the Japanese and English editions of the same manga layered page by page on your device. Move the round lens, and that spot appears in the other language.

Page images are handled only on your device. We never send them to our servers.

DEMO

Try it in your browser

The demo page is a sample we drew ourselves. Move the pointer over the Japanese page, or drag a finger across it. The same spot of the English edition appears inside the circle.

The alignment is computed in the browser that opened this page. It finds matching points in the two sample images and fits the English page to the shape of the Japanese one. No image is sent anywhere.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to layer two editions

Language editions of the same manga are usually made from the same artwork. What differs is mainly the text in the speech balloons. kasanegi uses this to find the pages and the places where the drawings match.

  1. 1. Match the pages

    kasanegi does not assume the pages are in the same order. For each English page, it picks the 8 Japanese pages with the closest small signature (64 bits). It then tries to align the drawings of each of these pages. Among the pairs that align, it keeps the one with the most matching points. Pages that exist in only one edition, such as covers or extras, are left without a partner.

  2. 2. Align by the drawings

    It matches feature points (SIFT) on the pages. The balloon text differs between editions, so small dark areas that look like text are not used for matching. From the matched points, it estimates a projective transform for the whole page with MAGSAC. The transform is accepted only when the corners of the mapped page, its area ratio and the spread of the points all look right.

  3. 3. Show only inside the circle

    The transform maps the other edition onto the shape of the page you are reading. Only the inside of the lens shows it. Outside the circle, you still see the edition you are reading. You can check just the balloons you are curious about in the other language.

The page-matching core

A program that pairs the pages of two language editions and aligns their drawings. It is written in Python with OpenCV. Of the three steps above, it handles matching and alignment.

02 · PC

Kasane-yomi for PC (round loupe)

Enter the URLs of the edition you read and the reference edition, then open them. The app decides the partner of each page automatically. There is no step where you pick the partner page by hand. Move the cursor over the page you are reading, and the same spot of the other edition appears inside the circle.

You can change the circle size and switch to black and white. You move between pages by scrolling or by entering a page number.

03 · Android

Android app

An app for reading on a phone, written in Kotlin. Enter the URLs of the edition you read and the reference edition, then tap Start. The pages of the chapter are matched. Where you drag your finger, a round window shows the other edition. You can move to the previous or next page, and swap the two editions.

The latest prototype paired 60 pages from the two editions of one chapter (64 and 58 pages). None of the pairs was wrong. Two-page spreads are paired one half at a time, so there can be more pairs than pages.

RESULTS

Results on real editions

These results come from two language editions of manga that are on sale. The titles and images belong to their rights holders, so we do not show them on this page.

33pairs

The page-matching core searched 193 Japanese pages for the partners of 57 English pages, and paired 33 of them. None of the pairs was wrong. Pages without a partner found were not layered (October 5, 2026; every pair checked by eye).

47pages

Kasane-yomi for PC matched 47 pages to the right partner page automatically, from 63 pages of the reading edition and 58 of the reference edition. This was checked against a separate visual review (September 27, 2026).

60pairs

The latest Android prototype paired 60 pages from the two editions of one chapter. None of the pairs was wrong. On another title it made 54 pairs, again with none wrong (October 7, 2026; every pair checked by eye).

Our goal is that, within 10 seconds of entering the two URLs, the whole chapter is ready to read with the two editions layered.

PRIVACY

We do not keep images on a server

Manga images belong to the rights holders of each work. kasanegi handles images on your device only to show the two editions you opened yourself, one over the other.

  • ServerWe never send page images to our servers. Our servers do not hold any images.
  • DeviceImages stay in the device's memory only while they are shown layered.
  • StorageImages are not saved as files. In testing, the Android app's cache and display storage are checked at the end of each run. In 8 runs on October 7, 2026, no image file was found.
  • DemoThe demo on this page also runs only in your browser.
